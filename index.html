About SDEC

Developing people through practical skills and entrepreneurship.

SDEC - Skill Development Entrepreneurship Company is focused on helping individuals develop practical, useful and entrepreneurial skills.

Our approach is centered around learning, practical development and opportunities that can help participants apply their knowledge in real-world situations.

We provide training programs designed to support personal development, entrepreneurship and skills acquisition.

Our Purpose

To equip people with relevant skills, encourage entrepreneurship and create pathways for individuals to develop their potential.
